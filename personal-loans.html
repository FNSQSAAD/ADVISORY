What Is a Personal Loan?

A personal loan is an unsecured loan. You borrow a set amount, then repay it over a fixed term, usually at a fixed interest rate. It's built for a defined purpose, not ongoing spending, which sets it apart from a credit card or line of credit.

We work with you to find a loan that matches your situation and your goals, not just the amount you're chasing.

Personal loans can cover:

  • A holiday
  • Home renovations
  • Debt consolidation
  • Medical bills
  • Refinancing an existing personal loan
  • A wedding
  • Other large personal purchases

Personal loans aren't approved for illegal purposes, business expenses, Lenders Mortgage Insurance (LMI), or a home loan deposit. If you need funding for one of these, we'll point you to the right option instead, such as a business loan, commercial loan, or a home loan, etc. Talk to us first, and we'll steer you toward the structure that actually fits.

Do You Qualify for a Personal Loan?

To apply, you'll generally need to:

  • Be at least 18 years old
  • Hold Australian citizenship, permanent residency, or an eligible visa
  • Have a regular, verifiable income
  • Have a fixed address in Australia
  • Meet the lender's minimum income requirement
  • Be employed or receive regular income
  • Not be in bankruptcy proceedings

If you are self-employed, verifiable income looks a little different for you, and that's exactly the kind of application we handle every day!
