Common Questions

Asked at almost every first call.

What does a mortgage broker actually cost me? +
In most cases, nothing directly. Lenders pay us a commission when your loan settles, and we disclose the amount before you commit to anything. If a fee ever applies to a complex matter, you’ll know before we begin — never after.
Will talking to you affect my credit score? +
No. The first call and the written strategy involve no credit check at all. An enquiry only appears on your file when you decide to proceed and an application is lodged — and we tell you before that happens.
How are you different from going to my bank? +
Your bank can only offer its own products. We compare your situation across a panel of more than forty lenders — banks, non-banks and specialists — and match it to the credit policy that suits it, not the one institution you happened to walk into.
I’m self-employed. Is that a problem? +
It’s a specialty, not a problem. Different lenders read ABN income, add-backs and retained profits very differently. We review your financials the way a credit assessor would, then shortlist the lenders whose policy actually fits your income shape.
How long does approval take? +
A well-prepared application typically runs from lodgement to unconditional approval in one to three weeks, depending on the lender and valuation. Half of all delays come from missing paperwork — which is why we verify the full file before anything is lodged.
What happens after settlement? +
We stay. Your loan gets a review every year — rate, structure and fit against the market — and you get a straight answer about whether it still deserves to hold your mortgage. Long-term relationships are the whole point of doing this well.
