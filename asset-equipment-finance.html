The Basics, Properly Explained

What Is Asset and Equipment Finance?

Asset finance lets you put equipment, vehicles, or technology to work in your business now, and pay for it over time, instead of finding a large amount upfront. It’s built to protect your cash flow while your business gets the productivity boost that new equipment brings.

“Asset finance” and “equipment finance” often get used as if they’re the same thing, but there’s a difference. Asset finance is the broader term. It covers vehicles, technology, and other equipment a growing business needs. Equipment finance sits inside that, covering the machinery your industry runs on, from heavy plants for mining to commercial kitchen gear for hospitality.

Put It to Work

What Can You Use Asset Finance For?

Use asset finance to acquire equipment, vehicles or technology, and spread the cost over manageable monthly repayments instead of one upfront hit.

Structured well, asset financing keeps cash in the business for necessary expenses: wages, stock, marketing, or the next opportunity. Small and medium Australian businesses use asset finance across almost every sector for exactly this reason. It lets growth happen without draining the account first.

Depending on your situation, an asset loan can be secured against the item you’re buying or against another asset you already own. We work out which structure suits your business before recommending anything.
