Complaints Policy

Last updated: 7 September 2026

Our Commitment

We want to know if something’s gone wrong. Every complaint gets looked at properly, and we use what we learn to do better. This policy explains how to make a complaint, what happens after you do, and what to do if you’re not satisfied with our response.

What Counts as a Complaint

A complaint is any expression of dissatisfaction about our services, our staff, or a decision we’ve made, where you’d like us to look into it or do something about it. This can relate to a loan application, advice you’ve received, how we’ve communicated with you, or anything else connected to your dealings with Finance Square Group.

How to Lodge a Complaint

We hope you’re delighted with our services. If you’re not, please notify your credit assistance provider first, so we get the chance to put it right directly:

  • Email: info@fnsq.com.au

Explain the details of your complaint as clearly as you can. You can do this verbally or in writing, and you don’t need to use the word “complaint” for us to treat it as one.

What Happens Next

When we receive a complaint, we’ll attempt to resolve it promptly. If your complaint is going to take longer than 30 days to resolve, we’ll notify you in writing to let you know.

Our Internal Dispute Resolution Scheme

If your complaint can’t be resolved directly with your credit assistance provider, please contact our Internal Complaints Officer:

Our External Dispute Resolution Scheme

If we don’t reach an agreement on your complaint through our internal process, you can refer it to our External Dispute Resolution (EDR) provider, the Australian Financial Complaints Authority (AFCA). AFCA is the single ASIC-approved external dispute resolution scheme for the Australian credit industry. AFCA provides fair and independent financial services complaint resolution that is free to consumers.

AFCA contact details

  • Phone: 1800 931 678 (call, 9am–5pm AEST/AEDT weekdays)
  • Online: afca.org.au
  • Mail: GPO Box 3, Melbourne VIC 3001

There’s no cost to you to lodge a complaint with AFCA. You can also request further details about our dispute resolution procedures, and about our Privacy Policy, at any time.
