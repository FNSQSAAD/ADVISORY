Government Help for First Home Buyers, Explained Simply

There’s genuine help available. The tricky part is knowing what you actually qualify for. Here’s the short version.

  • First Home Policies - Buy with as little as a 5% deposit and skip Lenders Mortgage Insurance entirely, backed by the government. Income caps and yearly place limits were recently removed, so more buyers now qualify than in previous years. Property price caps still apply and vary by location.
  • Help to Buy: A shared equity scheme where the government co-owns part of your home, which can lower your deposit to as little as 2%. Income and property price caps apply, and places are limited each year.
  • Stamp duty concessions: Most states and territories offer a full or partial stamp duty exemption for first home buyers, though thresholds differ everywhere. We’ll check exactly what applies where you’re buying.
  • First Home Super Saver Scheme: You may be able to use voluntary super contributions to build your deposit faster, with some tax advantages. We’ll tell you if it’s worth it for your situation.

Common Questions From First Home Buyers

Do I really need a 20% deposit?+
No. Some lenders and government schemes accept a deposit as low as 2-5%. We’ll show you what’s realistic for your situation on a strategy call.
Will using a broker cost me anything?+
In most cases, no. Brokers are typically paid a commission by the lender once your loan settles, not by you.
I’m self-employed, casual, or on a visa. Can I still buy?+
Possibly, yes. These situations are common and don’t automatically rule you out. It depends on your specific numbers, which is exactly the kind of application we handle every day.
Will checking my Finance Square Score hurt my credit score?+
No. Getting your Finance Square Score doesn’t involve a credit check. We only run a formal credit check once you decide to move forward with an application.
What if my parents or partner are helping with the deposit?+
Totally normal, and we can factor that into your plan, including guarantor options if relevant.
How is this different from just going to my bank?+
A bank can only offer you their own products. We compare options across multiple lenders and government schemes to find what actually fits you.
What happens after I submit the quiz?+
