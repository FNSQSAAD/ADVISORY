How this is calculated. Your income is converted to an after-tax figure using the ATO resident tax rates plus the 2% Medicare levy, split across two earners where your household has two or more people. Living expenses are the higher of what you entered and a benchmark for your household size, which is how lenders treat them. The repayment is then tested at your chosen rate plus a 3.00 percentage point serviceability buffer over 30 years, in line with APRA guidance, and 95% of your surplus is treated as available. The range reflects how widely lender policy varies.
