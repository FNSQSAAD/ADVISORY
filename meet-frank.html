Straight talk

What Frank won’t do — and why

Frank knows a lot, but he isn’t your broker. Some things are regulated credit assistance under the National Consumer Credit Protection Act, and only a licensed person who has looked at your whole situation can do them. So Frank won’t:

  • Quote you an interest rate or a repayment
  • Estimate how much you can borrow
  • Tell you whether you’ll be approved
  • Recommend a lender or a loan product

That’s Priya’s job. Priya Dey is Credit Representative 543438 of BLSSA Pty Ltd, Australian Credit Licence 391237.

What Priya receives

Frank gets you to Priya quickly, with your enquiry already written up, so your strategy call starts where you left off.

  • Your name and your mobile, confirmed back to you twice
  • What you’re looking to do and your rough timeline
  • Your questions and Frank’s answers, from the call transcript
  • Your booked time, or when you’d like her to call

Questions about Frank

Is Frank a real person?+
No. Frank is an AI assistant, and he’ll always tell you so at the start of the call. Priya Dey is the broker you’ll speak with on your strategy call.
Do I need a phone number to talk to Frank?+
No. Frank works in your web browser — you just need a microphone. He’ll only ask for your mobile if you’d like to book a call or have Priya call you back.
Does it cost anything?+
Talking to Frank is free, and so is the 15-minute strategy call. Our service to you is free — lender fees and government charges can still apply to a loan.
Can Frank tell me how much I can borrow?+
No. That depends on your full financial situation, so it’s a conversation for Priya. For a rough guide in the meantime, try our calculators.
It says my microphone is blocked. What now?+
Allow microphone access from your browser’s address bar and try again. Some apps’ built-in browsers, like Facebook’s and Instagram’s, block the microphone — open this page in Safari or Chrome instead. Or call us on 0495 040 500.
What if I’d rather speak to a person?+
No problem at all. Call us on 0495 040 500, or book a time online.
