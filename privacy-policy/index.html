2. Why We Collect Your Personal Information

We collect personal information so that we can provide credit assistance services, including:

  • assessing your borrowing capacity and your financial needs and objectives
  • identifying lenders and credit products that may suit your circumstances
  • preparing and submitting credit applications on your behalf
  • managing your loan from application through to settlement and afterwards
  • responding to enquiries you send us through this website or by phone, email or SMS
  • meeting our obligations under the National Consumer Credit Protection Act 2009 and other laws

If you do not give us the information we ask for, we may not be able to assess your application or provide credit assistance.

3. What Information We Collect

Depending on how you deal with us, we may collect:

  • your name, date of birth and contact details
  • your current and previous residential addresses
  • your employment and income information
  • your expenses, liabilities and assets
  • identification documents
  • your tax file number, only where we are required or permitted by law to collect it
  • other financial information needed to assess your application

When you use this website we may also collect your IP address, browser type, the pages you view and the date and time of your visit.

4. How We Collect It

We usually collect personal information directly from you, in person, by phone, by email or SMS, or through forms and booking tools on this website.

We may also collect information about you from lenders, credit reporting bodies, your accountant or other professional adviser, your employer, and publicly available sources, where you have authorised us to do so or where the law allows it.

5. Who We May Disclose Your Information To

We may share your personal information with:

  • lenders, banks and other credit providers
  • BLSSA Pty Ltd, our licensee, and Loan Market Group
  • credit reporting bodies, including Equifax, illion and Experian
  • lenders mortgage insurers
  • valuers, solicitors and conveyancers
  • government agencies, where required or authorised by law
  • our professional service providers, including our information technology, compliance and legal advisers

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not disclose it for another organisation’s marketing purposes.

6. Credit Reporting

With your authorisation, we and our licensee BLSSA Pty Ltd may obtain your consumer credit report and your commercial credit report from a credit reporting body, for the purpose of assessing your application and providing credit assistance.

Credit reporting bodies may include information about you in reports they provide to other credit providers, to assist those providers in assessing your credit worthiness.

7. Overseas Disclosure

Some of the parties we deal with are located overseas, or use technology platforms hosted overseas. Loan Market Group operates platforms that may store data on servers in countries including the United States and countries in the European Union. Our customer relationship system and our email and messaging providers may also process data outside Australia.

Before disclosing personal information to an overseas recipient, we take steps that are reasonable in the circumstances to ensure the recipient handles it in a way consistent with the Australian Privacy Principles.

8. Cookies and Website Analytics

This website uses cookies, which are small text files stored on your device. Cookies are used to keep the site working correctly, to remember your preferences, and to gather anonymous statistics about how the site is used.

We use Google Analytics for anonymous reporting of site usage. You can opt out of Google Analytics using the browser add-on at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

You can disable or delete cookies through your browser settings. Some parts of this website may not work correctly if you do.

9. Email, SMS and Phone Contact

If you give us your contact details through this website, request a call back, book an appointment, or become a client, you consent to us contacting you by email, SMS and phone about your enquiry and about our credit assistance services.

Every marketing email we send includes an unsubscribe link. Every marketing SMS we send includes a reply keyword to stop. You can also ask us to stop contacting you at any time by calling 0450 355 604 or emailing info@fnsq.com.au. We will action your request promptly.

We will continue to contact you about a loan application that is already underway, because that contact is necessary for the service you have asked us to provide.

10. How We Hold and Protect Your Information

We hold your personal information in electronic systems and, where relevant, in paper files. We take steps that are reasonable in the circumstances to protect it from misuse, interference, loss, and unauthorised access, modification or disclosure. Those steps include access controls, encrypted connections to this website, and agreements with our service providers.

We keep your information for as long as we need it for the purposes described in this policy, and for as long as the law requires us to keep it. When we no longer need it, we take reasonable steps to destroy it or to de-identify it.

11. Access and Correction

You may ask us for access to the personal information we hold about you, and you may ask us to correct it if it is inaccurate, out of date, incomplete, irrelevant or misleading.

To make a request, contact Priya Dey on 0450 355 604 or at info@fnsq.com.au. We will respond within a reasonable period. In some cases the law allows us to refuse access or correction. If that happens, we will tell you why in writing.

12. Complaints

If you think we have breached the Australian Privacy Principles or mishandled your personal information, please contact us first on 0450 355 604 or at info@fnsq.com.au. We will acknowledge your complaint and tell you how we intend to deal with it.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au or on 1300 363 992.

If your complaint relates to credit assistance, you may also contact the Australian Financial Complaints Authority at afca.org.au or on 1800 931 678.

13. Our Full APP Policy

Our full Australian Privacy Principles policy, maintained with our licensee, is available at lmg.broker/consumer-privacy.

We may update this policy from time to time. The version published on this page is the current one, and the date at the top shows when it was last updated.
